## What belongs here

Technical validation, data-audit findings, pilot outcomes, and negative or inconclusive findings. Separate them clearly. A passing calculation check does not demonstrate educational benefit; a satisfied user does not demonstrate improved comprehension.

Publish only evidence you can support. Do not replace missing results with expected outcomes.
